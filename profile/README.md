# Poke Alerts

Real-time restock and price alerts for Pokémon TCG collectors. Over 25,000 members use us to catch drops across 50+ retailers before they sell out.

[poke-alerts.com](https://poke-alerts.com) · [Join the Discord](https://poke-alerts.com/discord)

## What we build

- Restock alerts from more than 2,000 live monitors covering every major retailer
- Price drops and deals across 50+ retailers worldwide
- Product intel: verified allocations and release dates from retail partners
- An in-store stock checker for local inventory
- Region and store filtering, so you only hear about drops you can actually buy
- Weekly giveaways for members

Members have posted over 37,000 successful checkouts in our `#buy-success` channel.

Most of our repositories are private. The public ones live here.
